Before you call

How to read your tire size

Find this code on the sidewall of your tire (or on the sticker in the driver's door jamb). Read it to us over the phone and we can quote the right tire.

215/60R16

215
Tire width in millimeters
60
Sidewall height, as a percent of the width
R
Radial construction (almost every car tire)
16
Wheel (rim) diameter in inches

This is an example size. Yours may be different — read it right off your tire.

When to check your tires

Four signs it's time.

  • The penny test

    Put a penny in the tread with Lincoln's head down. If you can see all of his head, the tread is worn low.

  • Uneven wear

    One edge worn more than the other can point to alignment or suspension trouble.

  • Shaking at speed

    A vibration through the wheel can mean a tire problem worth checking.

  • Bulges or cracks

    A bubble or deep crack on the sidewall means the tire should be looked at right away.
