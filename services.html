Brakes

Free brake inspection

Brakes are the one thing you can't put off. Squealing, grinding, a pulsing or soft pedal, or the car pulling to one side when you stop are all reasons to have them checked.

The brake inspection is free — it's on the banner on the front of the building. If they need work, you'll get an estimate before anything is done.

Signs it's time

  • Squealing or grinding when you stop
  • Pedal feels soft, low or pulses
  • Car pulls to one side under braking
  • Brake warning light on the dash

Check-engine diagnostics

Ask about a free check-engine scan

A check-engine light can mean something small or something that needs attention now. Guessing gets expensive.

We read the codes and look into what's causing them, then explain it in plain English or Spanish. Ask about a free check-engine scan when you call.

Call right away if

  • The light is flashing, not steady
  • The car is shaking or losing power
  • You smell fuel or see smoke

Suspension & lowering

Repairs and lowering installs

Clunks over bumps, a bouncy ride, or a car that sits uneven usually point to suspension. We handle suspension repairs in the shop.

Want your car sitting lower? We do lowering installs too. Call to talk through what you have in mind and get an estimate.

Common signs

  • Clunking over bumps or potholes
  • Too much bounce after a dip
  • One corner sits lower than the others
