Smart buyer checklist

Buy used with your eyes open.

Good advice for any used car, wherever you buy it. And since this is a repair shop too, the people who know cars are right here to answer questions.

  • Ask for the VIN and run a vehicle history report
  • Make sure the title is available and matches the VIN
  • Look at the tires and ask about the brakes
  • See it in daylight and start it cold if you can
  • Bring your driver's license and proof of insurance
