FAQ

Questions people ask

Don't see yours? Call (804) 233-5599.

Are you open on weekends?

Our card says we're open 7 days a week. Hours can change day to day, so call (804) 233-5599 to check today's hours before you come by.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Estimates are free, so you know what your car needs before any work starts.

Is the brake inspection really free?

Yes. Free brake inspection, just like the banner on the front of the shop says.

Do you sell used tires?

Yes, we sell both new and used tires. Call with your tire size (it's printed on the sidewall) and ask for a price.

How much will my repair cost?

Every car is different, so we start with a free estimate. Call (804) 233-5599 or stop by 5258 Hull Street Rd.

My check-engine light is on. Can you help?

Yes. We do diagnostics to find out why the light is on. Ask about a free check-engine scan when you call.

Do you have cars for sale?

Yes. What's on the lot changes, so call or text (804) 233-5599 for current inventory.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Our business card is in Spanish and this whole website is available in Spanish.

Where do I park?

There's a lot right in front of the shop at 5258 Hull Street Rd, on the Hull Street corridor in Southside Richmond.
